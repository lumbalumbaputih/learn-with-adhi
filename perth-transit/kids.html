    For grown-ups

    Perth Train Spotter is a kids' version of the Perth Transit Map. It shows where Perth's trains and the ferry should be, worked out from the published timetable.

    It is not live. Trains are not tracked, so delays and cancellations are not shown. Please don't use it to plan a real trip; check the official journey planner instead. How long trains stop at stations and how they speed up and slow down are our estimates.

    Settings

    This browser can't read aloud.

    Only voices installed on this device are used, never online ones, so nothing is read aloud if there is no English voice here. These choices are kept on this device only.

    Privacy

    No location, no accounts and no names. Nothing your child does here leaves this device. Visits to the page are counted with Vercel Web Analytics, which does not use cookies, and the map pictures are loaded from OpenFreeMap.

    Data

    Timetable data from the Public Transport Authority of Western Australia. This data is available free of charge from www.transperth.wa.gov.au.

    Basemap by OpenFreeMap, using OpenMapTiles and data from OpenStreetMap contributors.
