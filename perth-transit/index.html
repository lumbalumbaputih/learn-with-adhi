About this map

This map shows where buses and trains should be, worked out from the published timetable. It is not live. Vehicles are not tracked by GPS, so delays, cancellations and replacement buses are not shown.

Use it to see how the network moves through the day. Check the official journey planner for live times before you travel.

Data

Timetable data from the Public Transport Authority of Western Australia. This data is available free of charge from www.transperth.wa.gov.au.

Basemap by OpenFreeMap, using OpenMapTiles and data from OpenStreetMap contributors.

This is an independent project. It is not affiliated with or endorsed by the Public Transport Authority or any transport operator.
